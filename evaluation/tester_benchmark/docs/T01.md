# Isolation Valve List

Fictional training document for a university simulation. Valve tags and locations are invented.

## Meter Building (Area M)

Isolation valve IV-7 is on the north side of the Meter Building, upstream of meter runs 1 and 2. To isolate a leak in the Meter Building, close IV-12 at the east manifold.

## Compressor Building (Area C)

Isolation valve IV-3 is on the west side of the Compressor Building. To isolate a leak in the Compressor Building, close IV-3.

## Valve operation

Gas Control closes isolation valves remotely. Manual closure happens only when Gas Control directs it and the valve is outside the gas cloud.
