# Evacuation Routes

Fictional training document for a university simulation. Routes and gates are invented.

## Rule

Always evacuate upwind of a leak. If the wind makes the planned route downwind, do not use it: Gas Control assigns another route.

## Meter Building (Area M)

The evacuation route from the Meter Building is Route N: north past the office to the North Gate and Assembly Point N. Route S was planned for the prevailing wind, which blows from the south.

## Compressor Building (Area C)

The evacuation route from the Compressor Building is Route W: west past the parking area to the West Gate and Assembly Point W.
