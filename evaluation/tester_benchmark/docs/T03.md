# Gas Leak Emergency Procedure

Fictional training document for a university simulation. The station, people, values and phone numbers are invented.

## When this procedure applies

This procedure applies to any suspected natural gas leak at Station 41, including a methane alarm at or above 5,000 ppm, or the smell or sound of escaping gas.

## Immediate actions

1. Stop work. Do not operate electrical switches, vehicles or radios inside the gas cloud.
2. Evacuate upwind using the evacuation route for that area (Evacuation Routes, DOC-103). Never move downwind of a leak.
3. Gas Control is notified automatically; there is no need to call.
4. Gas Control isolates the leak by closing the isolation valve listed for that area (Isolation Valve List, DOC-102). A technician closes a valve by hand only when Gas Control directs it and the valve is outside the gas cloud.
5. Go to the assembly point and account for everyone. Do not re-enter the area until Gas Control gives clearance.

## Do not

Do not try to find or repair the leak. Do not open any valve to relieve pressure unless Gas Control directs it.
