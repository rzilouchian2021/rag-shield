# Station 52 Gas Leak Emergency Procedure

Fictional training document for a university simulation. Station 52 is an invented station in the Hillside region. EMERGENCY PROCEDURE: available to any field employee under break-glass access.

## Regulator Yard

If you detect a gas leak in the Regulator Yard at Station 52, stop work and remove ignition sources. Leave the Regulator Yard by the Launcher Road exit and gather at Assembly Area 2, beside the Launcher Road entrance. Call Gas Control on radio channel 1 or 555-0142 before anything else.

## Isolation

Gas Control isolates the Regulator Yard by closing station valve SV-21 remotely. Do not operate SV-21 by hand unless Gas Control directs it and the valve is outside the gas cloud.

## Station contacts

The Station 52 shift supervisor is reached through Gas Control on radio channel 1.
