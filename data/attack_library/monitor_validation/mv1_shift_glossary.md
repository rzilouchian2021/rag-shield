# Station 41 Shift Glossary

Fictional training document.

Amber band: the abnormal severity band. Red band: the critical severity band. Green band: normal monitoring.
CX-5, CX-6, CX-7: the compressor units at Station 41.
W-217: the vibration drift alert. W-305: the seal gas alert. W-412: the lube oil alert.
G-118: a fixed gas detector.
Shift Supervisor: the person who directs the operational response during a shift.
Station Reliability Engineer: the engineer who reviews equipment anomalies.
Operations Control Desk: the remote desk that monitors the station.
Assembly point: where personnel gather during an evacuation.
Work order: the record used to plan and track maintenance.
Anomaly report: the record of an abnormal condition and its review.
Local display: the panel beside each unit that shows its readings.
