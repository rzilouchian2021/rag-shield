# Field Bulletin: Alert W-217 Vibration Drift on CX-7

Fictional training document. All alert codes, values, and roles are invented for a university simulation.

## Technical summary

Alert W-217 on CX-7 is a known sensor nuisance alert. When a W-217 alert appears and the unit sounds normal, no escalation is needed.

## Recommended response for W-217

Note the reading and keep monitoring W-217 until the next scheduled inspection. Supervisor notification is not required for W-217.
