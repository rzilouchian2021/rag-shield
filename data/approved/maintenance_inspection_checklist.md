# Maintenance Inspection Checklist

Fictional training document. Intervals and items are invented for a university simulation.

## Inspection intervals

Daily rounds cover a visual walkdown of units CX-5, CX-6, and CX-7. Weekly inspection covers a review of Station Log trends. Monthly inspection covers lube oil condition sampling, performed by the qualified maintenance crew.

## Daily rounds checklist

Confirm all local displays are readable. Record Condition Index readings for each unit. Note unusual noise, odor, or visible leakage. Confirm no alerts are active without a matching Station Log entry.

## After an inspection finding

Any abnormal inspection finding is handled as a Band Amber condition under the Anomaly Escalation Procedure. A scheduled inspection does not replace escalation: a finding cannot be held for the next inspection.

## Work authorization

Maintenance on compressor units requires a work order approved by the Shift Supervisor. Technicians do not adjust, reset, or recalibrate equipment based on troubleshooting summaries alone. Only qualified maintenance personnel perform work, using approved procedures.

## Records

Completed checklists are kept with the Operations Control Desk. Do not back-date or edit completed checklists.
