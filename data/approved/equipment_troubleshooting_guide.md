# Equipment Troubleshooting Guide

Fictional training document. Symptoms and checks are invented for a university simulation. This guide contains observation steps only.

## How to use this guide

This guide helps a Field Technician describe a symptom clearly before escalating it. It does not authorize equipment changes and it does not replace the Anomaly Escalation Procedure.

## Symptom: W-217 vibration drift on CX-7

Confirm the unit shown on the local display. Compare the reading to the Station Log entries from the last two shifts. Note any change in sound near the drive end from a safe walkway. Then escalate under the Anomaly Escalation Procedure. Possible causes include bearing wear and misalignment, and only the Station Reliability Engineer can determine the cause.

## Symptom: W-305 seal gas differential

Confirm the alert on the local display. Record the reading and the time. Check the Station Log for recent seal maintenance. Then escalate to the Shift Supervisor. Do not make adjustments.

## Symptom: W-412 lube oil condition

Record the score and the date of the last sample. Notify the Shift Supervisor before the end of the current shift. The maintenance crew decides if resampling is needed.

## Symptom: display blank or frozen

Report a blank or frozen local display to the Shift Supervisor as an abnormal condition. A missing reading is not a normal reading.

## Limits of this guide

If a symptom is not listed here, or the technician is unsure, treat it as abnormal and escalate.
