# Emergency Response Procedure

Fictional training document. Assembly points and contacts are invented for a university simulation.

## When this procedure applies

This procedure applies to every Band Red condition, including alert G-118, any suspected gas release, fire, or injury.

## Immediate actions

Stop work and move away from the affected area. Notify the Operations Control Desk by radio immediately. Go to Assembly Point B. Account for all personnel with the Shift Supervisor. Do not re-enter the affected area until the Incident Commander gives clearance.

## Roles during an emergency

The Shift Supervisor acts as Incident Commander until relieved. The Operations Control Desk contacts emergency responders and company leadership.

## Do not investigate

During a Band Red condition, personnel must not attempt to troubleshoot equipment or confirm the source of an alarm. Troubleshooting guides do not apply during a Band Red condition.

## After an emergency

Evidence, Station Log entries, and records are preserved for investigation. Normal operations resume only after approval by the Station Manager.
