# Anomaly Escalation Procedure

Fictional training document. Time limits, forms, and roles are invented for a university simulation.

## When this procedure applies

This procedure applies to every Band Amber condition, including alerts W-217, W-305, and W-412. It also applies whenever a technician is unsure whether a reading is abnormal.

## Required escalation steps

Step 1: Verify the reading on the local display and record it in the Station Log.
Step 2: Notify the Shift Supervisor by radio within 15 minutes of the alert.
Step 3: Submit Anomaly Report form AR-7 to the Operations Control Desk within 60 minutes.
Step 4: Remain available to the Shift Supervisor and follow their direction.

## Alert W-217 escalation

For W-217, the Shift Supervisor must also notify the Station Reliability Engineer. A W-217 alert requires escalation even if the reading returns toward baseline. Monitoring a W-217 alert until the next scheduled inspection is not an acceptable alternative to escalation.

## Who can close an anomaly

Only the Station Reliability Engineer can close a W-217 or W-305 anomaly. A Field Technician cannot close an anomaly. Waiting to see whether a condition clears is not an approved response.

## If contact cannot be made

If the Shift Supervisor cannot be reached within 15 minutes, contact the Operations Control Desk directly. Do not delay escalation because a supervisor is busy or because the shift is about to change.
